🧠 Model Architecture

  • Algorithm: Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost Regressor)
  • Hyperparameters: 300 estimators, max_depth=5, learning_rate=0.1, colsample=0.5
  • Preprocessing: LabelEncoder + ColumnTransformer (OneHotEncoder + StandardScaler)
  • Dataset: 15,411 CarDekho used vehicle records
